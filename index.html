That day on foot

How the distances work

Straight-line miles from the hotel at L'Enfant Plaza, which is why the Mall museums are a stroll and the Cathedral is a ride. Every hop is costed as an UberXL — about 18 mph door to door, plus a few minutes standing on a corner with your hands in your pockets. The scheduler keeps a day's stops near each other where it can, and Checks says something when a day turns into a lot of riding. None of it is a rule. It just means nobody is surprised at nine at night.
